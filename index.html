Melto Run

Online Multiplayer

The definitive platforming experience has arrived. We have combined high-speed parkour mechanics, intense competitive racing, and deep environmental storytelling into one massive online world. Take control of advanced cybernetic avatars and outrun your opponents across shifting, perilous landscapes.

  • 64-Player Battle Races
  • Customizable Cyborg Avatars
  • Dynamic Environmental Hazards
  • Cross-Platform Matchmaking
  • Global Ranked Leaderboards
  • Weekly Event Challenges

Precision Platforming

Master frame-perfect jumps, wall-runs, and grappling mechanics. The physics engine is tuned for speed and extreme responsiveness, rewarding skill and punishing hesitation.

Tactical Power-Ups

Deploy EMP blasts, speed boosts, and trap modules mid-run. Item management is crucial to securing first place against global competitors.

Endless Biomes

From neon-drenched cyberpunk skylines to overgrown toxic ruins, the procedural track generation ensures no two multiplayer runs ever feel exactly the same.
